How I Work

From Idea to Shipped

Every project — including this site — goes through the same five steps, built entirely from a phone.

01

Discover

Start with the actual problem, not the solution. For LifeFlow that meant noticing I was juggling three different apps just to plan a day.

02

Sketch

Rough out the layout and flow in notes or on paper before writing a single line — structure decisions are cheap to change here, expensive later.

03

Build

One module at a time, in Spck Editor, on my phone. Each piece gets tested on a real day before the next one starts.

04

Refine

This is where most of the actual time goes — hover states, transitions, edge cases, and going back over things until they feel right.

05

Ship

Deploy, share it, and actually use it — real use is what surfaces the next round of things worth fixing.
